# Learning pack sources and licenses

Actual entries: 3000 words, 500 phrases, 100 dialogues. Armenian glosses: 98.

Vocabulary: Hermit Dave, FrequencyWords, 2018 English frequency list, derived from OPUS OpenSubtitles2018. Source: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords/master/content/2018/en/en_50k.txt
Repository and license statement: https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords#license
Content licensed CC BY-SA 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
Modifications: filtered single alphabetic words; selected 3,000 frequency-ranked entries; added estimated difficulty labels and a small original Armenian gloss set. These vocabulary adaptations remain CC BY-SA 4.0. This is a frequency word list, not a dictionary: most entries do not yet have definitions or Armenian equivalents. Rank-based levels are estimates, not validated CEFR levels. The subtitle-derived list may contain informal vocabulary and names.

Phrases and dialogues: original pattern-based practice material created for RoyalArm AI (20 practical actions, 25 phrase patterns, 5 four-turn dialogue patterns), released CC0 1.0: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/. No paid translation API was used to generate the corpus. No claims of human expert review.
